Frequently asked questions


What is JSON-LD?


It's a small block of code that describes your business in a format search engines read directly. Unlike visible text, it's structured: name, address, phone, hours and services are labelled, so Google doesn't have to guess.


Will schema get me to #1 on Google?


No. Schema helps search engines understand your page; it is not a ranking boost by itself. It can make you eligible for richer search results (like a knowledge panel or business details), but rankings still come from relevance, quality and links.


Do I need a plugin for this?


No. You can paste the generated code into any HTML page. If your site is on WordPress, a plugin can add it automatically, but the code is the same.


Is my data sent anywhere by this tool?


No. Everything happens in your browser. The form never uploads your business details to us or anyone else.
