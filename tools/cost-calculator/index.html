Frequently asked questions

What does a small business website usually cost?

Common ranges: a DIY site with a template and free hosting can be under $100 a year; a done-for-you small site typically runs a few hundred to a few thousand dollars one-time, plus the yearly costs. The exact number depends on pages, features and who writes the words.

Why is "your time" in the calculation?

Because it's a real cost. If you spend 20 hours building a site and your time is worth $25 an hour, that's $500 of effort on top of any money you pay. People often forget this and are surprised by how long DIY takes.

What are the ongoing costs?

Usually a domain (about $10–15 a year), hosting (free to a few hundred a year), and any website builder or plugin subscriptions. SSL certificates are usually free these days. Budget for the yearly total, not just the build.

Is this an official quote?

No. It's an estimate tool with editable inputs, not a quote for your specific project. For an exact price, email us and we'll give you a straight answer.
