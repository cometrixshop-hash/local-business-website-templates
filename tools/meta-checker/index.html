Frequently asked questions

What are the limits?

Google typically shows about 50–60 characters of a page title and about 150–160 characters of a meta description before truncating with an ellipsis. The exact cutoff varies by device and query, so staying under these is the safe target.

Does Google always use my meta description?

No. Google may rewrite it if it thinks another part of your page answers the search better. A well-written description still helps because it's often used as-is, and it's what people read when deciding whether to click.

Should my title start with my business name?

Usually put the topic first and your brand last, separated by a dash or pipe: Service in City | Business Name. That way the useful words appear before the cutoff. Put your brand first only if brand recognition is the main goal.

Is this tool sending my text anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your titles and descriptions never leave this page.
