Frequently asked questions

Why does mobile matter so much?

Because that's where your customers are. Most local searches — "plumber near me", "HVAC repair" — happen on a phone, often with an urgent need. A site that's hard to use on a phone loses the call before it starts.

What's the one thing that matters most?

A tap-to-call button. If someone can tap your number and dial without zooming or typing, you've already beaten a lot of competitor sites.

How do I test my real site?

Open it on your own phone. Check that you can call you with one tap, read the text without zooming, and that nothing scrolls sideways. Then run it through Google's PageSpeed Insights for a deeper look.

Is this a full accessibility audit?

No. It's a practical checklist for the most common mobile problems. A proper accessibility audit covers contrast, screen readers, keyboard navigation and more — worth doing, but a separate job.
