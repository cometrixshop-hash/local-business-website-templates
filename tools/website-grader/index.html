    Frequently asked questions

    How does the automatic check work?

    This is a static site, so there is no server doing the checking. Your browser fetches your page and analyses its HTML. Because most websites do not allow cross-origin requests from a browser, the tool tries your site directly first and then falls back to free public relays (Jina Reader, AllOrigins, CodeTabs). If all of them fail, the self-checklist above takes over.

    Why did it fail to fetch my site?

    Relays can be slow or rate-limited, and some sites (heavy JavaScript apps, sites behind firewalls or CDNs, login pages) do not return usable HTML to automated requests. That is not your fault and does not mean your site is broken. Use the checklist above, or run PageSpeed Insights for a deeper look.

    What counts as a good score?

    80 or above is a solid foundation. 50-79 means real room to improve. Below 50 means serious issues are likely costing you calls. The score is a first pass — a high score does not guarantee a good site, and a low score always deserves a closer look.

    Does this send my URL anywhere?

    Yes, to fetch your page it must be requested by a relay service (or directly by your browser when the site allows it). Nothing is stored by us, and no account or sign-up is involved. If that matters for you, use the self-checklist instead.

    Is this a full audit?

    No. It reads one HTML page and cannot judge your real loading speed, tap-target sizes, colour contrast, screen-reader behaviour, or content quality. It is an automated first pass, not a full audit.
