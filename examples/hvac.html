HVAC / Heating & Cooling Website Examples

Service, maintenance plans and financing, built around a fast call. 3 real, live hvac / heating & cooling websites, reviewed and critiqued. Third-party sites — not our clients.

Carini Home Services

San Diego, CA · https://www.carinihome.com/ · loads in 0.32s

Why it is here: Carries a 4.8-star rating with 812 reviews in its structured data, and a contact form with a service-type dropdown.

What it does well

  • The hero names San Diego and the exact services, so a visitor knows immediately they are in the right place.
  • A contact form asks for the type of service, which helps the office route the lead.
  • Financing is mentioned, which matters for big-ticket HVAC replacements.

What could be better

  • The homepage is dense — 4,400+ words and 73 images — so it can feel busy on a phone.
  • No prices or maintenance-plan rates are published, which pushes price questions onto the form.

Command Comfort

Huntington Beach, CA · https://commandcomfort.com/ · loads in 1.31s

Why it is here: Shows its California contractor license (#942279) in the footer and offers a callback form plus a “which AC” quiz.

What it does well

  • The headline “Stop Checking the Weather, Command It.” is memorable and on-brand.
  • A callback form and an AC selector quiz give two low-friction ways to make contact.
  • Financing options are advertised, which helps close larger replacements.

What could be better

  • A cookie/privacy popup covers part of the hero on a first visit, which hides the headline.
  • A repeating promo ticker across the top makes the header feel busy.

Bob’s AC

Umatilla, FL · https://bobsac.com/ · loads in 0.29s

Why it is here: States “Trusted in Umatilla and Port Orange since 1977” and shows license #CAC1824586 in the footer.

What it does well

  • The “since 1977” line and real fleet photo build instant credibility.
  • Upfront pricing, free estimates and financing are all stated in the meta description and homepage.
  • One trusted team for AC, electrical, roofing and gutters is a clear cross-sell.

What could be better

  • No review rating or count appears in the page HTML.
  • The homepage is image-heavy (96 images), which can slow down mobile load on a weak connection.
