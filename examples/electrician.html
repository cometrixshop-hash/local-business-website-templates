Haas & Sons Electric

Columbia, MD · https://haasandsons.com · loads in 0.19s

Why it is here: Shows a 4.9 rating with 1,429 reviews in its structured data and lists eight Maryland locations.

What it does well

  • The 4.9/1,429 review count is exceptional social proof for a local trade.
  • A free home safety inspection offer lowers the barrier to a first call.
  • The service area is broken down by town (Columbia, Pasadena, Ellicott City, Bowie, Severn, Severna Park, Glen Burnie).

What could be better

  • The homepage is busy — a yellow promo strip, blue nav and large hero panel compete for attention.
  • No prices or financing are published.

Nipper Electric

Virginia Beach, VA · https://nipperelectric.com · loads in 0.61s

Why it is here: Has a contact form with first name, last name, email and phone fields, plus a “We Service” list of nearby cities.

What it does well

  • The “Family Owned and Operated” headline builds local trust.
  • The contact form captures enough detail for the office to follow up.
  • The footer lists Virginia Beach, Norfolk and Chesapeake service pages, which is good for local SEO.

What could be better

  • The hero is dark and low-contrast, which can be hard to read.
  • No review rating or count is visible in the page HTML.
