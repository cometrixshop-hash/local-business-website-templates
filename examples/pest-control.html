Free inspections and pest-by-pest answers. 2 real, live pest control websites, reviewed and critiqued. Third-party sites — not our clients.

Arrow Pest Control

Morganville, NJ · https://arrownj.com · loads in 0.20s

Why it is here: Has a free-quote form in the hero and two office addresses (Morganville and Summit, NJ) in the footer.

What it does well

  • The hero pairs a strong claim (“New Jersey’s Best Pest Control Company”) with a free-quote form.
  • A “Common New Jersey Pests We Control” section helps visitors identify their problem.
  • Two office locations with hours are listed in the footer.

What could be better

  • No review rating or count is visible in the page HTML.
  • The design is functional but not distinctive.

Aruza Pest Control

Charlotte, NC · https://aruzapest.com · loads in 0.79s

Why it is here: Explains a 5-step “Aruza 360°” process and lists branches across NC, SC, FL and TX.

What it does well

  • The 5-step process section explains how the service works before you buy.
  • The dark-green palette and certification badges feel professional.
  • A branch list with “Schedule Inspection” and “Start Service” links makes the next step clear.

What could be better

  • It is a multi-state company, so it feels less local than a single-town pest control business.
  • No prices are published.
