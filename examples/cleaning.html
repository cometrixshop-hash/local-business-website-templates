House & Office Cleaning Website Examples

Instant booking, pricing and real work galleries. 2 real, live house & office cleaning websites, reviewed and critiqued. Third-party sites — not our clients.

Emerald House Cleaning

Los Angeles, CA · https://www.losangeleshousecleaners.net/ · loads in 0.90s

Why it is here: Offers instant online booking and a pricing page, with a 4.7 rating from 50 reviews in its structured data.

What it does well

  • The “Book Online Now” call-to-action is repeated in the header and hero, and booking is instant.
  • A separate pricing page and service pages for apartments, Airbnb and move-in/move-out cover the main use cases.
  • The 10,000+ cleans claim and 4.7 rating give new visitors proof.

What could be better

  • It is a referral agency rather than an employed cleaning team, which the homepage could state more clearly.
  • The page is long and repeats the booking CTA many times.

Master Clean Bay Area

San Jose, CA · https://mastercleanbayarea.com/ · loads in 0.53s

Why it is here: Claims 30 years in business and transparent pricing, with a “Cities We Serve” page and a gallery.

What it does well

  • The dark, elegant hero and interior photography make it look more premium than a typical cleaning site.
  • “Transparent pricing” is in the meta description and the site has a quote request flow.
  • A gallery and post-construction portfolio show real work, not stock photos.

What could be better

  • No online booking — the quote request is a form, not a live calendar.
  • No review rating or count is visible in the page HTML.
