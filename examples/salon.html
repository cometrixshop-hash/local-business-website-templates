Hair Salon Website Examples

Portfolio-first design with a clear book button. 2 real, live hair salon websites, reviewed and critiqued. Third-party sites — not our clients.

The Lane Salon

San Francisco, CA · https://thelanesalon.com · loads in 0.60s

Why it is here: Has a visible “Book Now” button in the header and a stylist grid under “The Artists”.

What it does well

  • The editorial, neutral design lets the photography carry the brand.
  • A stylist grid (“The Artists”) helps clients choose a person, not just a service.
  • The address and phone are in the footer, and booking is one click from the header.

What could be better

  • The site is thin on text (under 200 words), so it gives search engines little to work with.
  • No prices are published, which is unusual for a salon.

Jet Rhys

Solana Beach, CA · https://jetrhys.com · loads in 0.74s

Why it is here: Uses a dark, high-fashion hero (“Cut + Color”) with a stylist roster and hours in the footer.

What it does well

  • The black-and-white photography and dark palette feel premium and memorable.
  • The stylist roster names every team member, which personalizes the experience.
  • Hours, location, contact and a book link are all in the footer.

What could be better

  • The meta description is empty, which is a missed SEO opportunity.
  • No prices are published on the homepage.
