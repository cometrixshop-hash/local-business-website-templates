How we picked these

Every site was visited and checked by hand: it has to be live, US-based, locally owned and genuinely well put together. We looked for a clear way to make contact (tap-to-call, a form or online booking), a stated service area, real photos and reviews where available, and a fast load on desktop. We excluded businesses we have already contacted.
