How We Picked These

This directory is a genuine review, not a list of links. Here is exactly what we did.

The criteria

Each site had to pass all of these before we included it:

  • Live and US-based. The site returns a 200 and is a real local business with a US address and phone number.
  • Locally owned. We preferred independent businesses over national franchises, so the examples feel achievable for a one-owner shop.
  • Genuinely well put together. It has to look designed in the last few years: readable type, a real layout, real photography (not a page of clip art), and a clear way to make contact.
  • A clear contact path. A tap-to-call number, a contact form, or online booking — ideally more than one.
  • A stated service area. The towns or counties it serves are named somewhere on the site.
  • Fast enough. We measured load time on desktop. Every example loads in under 1.5 seconds in our test.

How we verified each one

  • We visited the site and confirmed it loads (HTTP 200).
  • We read the homepage and footer for the phone number, address, hours and service area.
  • We checked the HTML for tap-to-call links (tel:), contact forms, review schema and pricing pages.
  • We measured the load time.
  • We took a screenshot of the homepage on a desktop browser. The thumbnails in this directory are crops of those screenshots.

What we excluded

  • Businesses we have already contacted. We keep an outreach log and checked it before selecting any example.
  • National franchises and marketplaces, where the local page is a template the owner cannot control.
  • Sites that looked dated, broken, or were little more than a parked domain.

Honest critiques

Every example lists 2–3 things the site does well and 1–2 things it could improve. The critiques are ours, based on what we could see on the live site. We are not affiliated with any business listed, and we were not paid to include them.
